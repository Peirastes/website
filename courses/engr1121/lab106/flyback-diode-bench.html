2N2222: pin 9 (5 V) through 1 kΩ gives IB ≈ 4.3 mA, so with hFE ≈ 100 it passes at most about 0.43 A. VCE(sat) 0.3 V at 150 mA, 1 V at 500 mA; storage 225 ns, fall 60 ns; VCEO 40 V; 625 mW (datasheet values). Assumed, with no datasheet for the kit motor: R = 4 Ω, L = 2 mH, K = 0.02 V·s/rad (no-load about 4000 rpm at 9 V); the collector breaks down at 60 V (between VCEO 40 V and VCBO 75 V); 20 pF at the collector.
