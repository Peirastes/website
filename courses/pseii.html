About This Course

The second semester of calculus-based physics for scientists and engineers: electric charge and fields, electric potential and capacitance, current and DC circuits, magnetism, and electromagnetic induction. These are the complete lecture notes, chapter by chapter, with interactive simulations embedded alongside the concepts they illustrate — an open reference for anyone working through the subject.
