About This Lab

A first-year, hands-on robotics lab. The semester moves from the physical to the programmable: mechanisms, gears and linkages; CAD, 3D printing and rapid prototyping; circuits and measurement; programming the Arduino Uno; sensors and logic; DC and servo motors — and then a team project that brings them together. Each lab page here describes what the lab sets out to do, and where a bench procedure benefits from rehearsal, a browser simulator lets you run it before you wire it.
