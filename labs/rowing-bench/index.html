Overview

An auto-rower pulls the handle (force–velocity line F = Fmax(1 − v/vmax)). The rope turns the spool; the one-way clutch (amber when locked) drives the belt and the motor; the flywheel coasts through the recovery while the recoil spring rewinds the rope. Every motion is the realistic plant of sim/rowing_plant.py, on the placeholder constants.
