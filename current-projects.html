Current Projects

Below are the projects currently in development or active exploration. These range from foundational investigations in geometric reasoning and interactive visualization platforms to advanced research in control systems, energy conversion, and dynamical systems analysis.

Academic & Educational

Geometrical Inference

Exploring the intersection of geometry, logic, and inference. Investigating how geometric relationships and spatial reasoning can rigorously test and falsify claims about mathematical structures and underlying principles.

Active

Manim Animations

Creating mathematical animations using Manim (Mathematical Animation Engine) to visualize and clarify complex concepts in physics, dynamical systems, and calculus. Developing visual frameworks that make mathematical structures accessible and their behavior transparent.

Active

Harmonic Oscillators

Comprehensive investigation of harmonic oscillator systems across multiple domains: mechanical pendulums, electrical circuits, and quantum systems. Exploring resonance, damping, and coupling phenomena.

Active

Dynamic Control of an Aeropendulum

Advanced feedback control systems research using a propeller-driven pendulum testbed. Integrating sensor fusion, real-time control algorithms, and visualization to investigate nonlinear dynamics and control synthesis in physical systems.

Active

Resonance Structures in Acoustic Systems

Theoretical and experimental study of standing waves and resonance phenomena in acoustic tubes and cavities. Connecting classical wave theory to real-world sound physics and musical acoustics.

Active

Personal Research & Engineering

Axial Flux Generator Design

Engineering and optimization of axial flux permanent magnet generators for energy conversion applications. Exploring mechanical design, electromagnetic efficiency, and power output under different operating conditions.

In Development

Solar Power System Integration

Design and implementation of a complete solar power system including panel optimization, charge management, energy storage, and load balancing. Quantifying efficiency across the entire power conversion chain.
