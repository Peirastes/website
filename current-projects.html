Current Projects

Below are active projects in production and advanced development, alongside investigations in progress. These span interactive visualization platforms, educational tools, research systems, and explorations in physics, dynamical systems, and engineering.

Active

Electrostatics Lab

An interactive 3D web-based visualization tool for exploring electric fields, equipotential surfaces, and field lines. Supports 11 charge configurations (point charges, dipoles, extended objects, and plate geometries). Features real-time RK4 field line integration with advanced spherical coordinate seeding, vector field glyphs, 3D equipotential surfaces via marching cubes, 2D slice views with heat maps, and a camera-aligned live cross-section.

In Progress

Dynamical Systems Lab

An interactive React-based laboratory for exploring and visualizing dynamical systems. Includes implementations of classical systems like Lorenz attractors, pendulums, oscillators, and bifurcation phenomena. Provides real-time 3D visualization and parameter adjustment for understanding complex system behavior.

In Progress

Eisenhower Task Manager

Web-based productivity application implementing the Eisenhower Decision Matrix for strategic prioritization. Features 4-quadrant matrix view, list visualization, Gantt timeline with history tracking, and Planning/Execution Score for measuring estimation accuracy. Multiple visualization modes enable flexible task management workflows.

In Progress

ECDO Watch

Real-time geophysics monitoring system tracking Earth-system anomalies across four independent data channels: solar wind energy input (Kp index), planetary rotation rate (LOD), ground magnetic fields, and gravity harmonics. Implements falsification-first methodology with quiet-day gating and multi-channel coherence detection to distinguish signal from noise.

In Progress

SPECTRUM Market Analytics

Multi-dimensional market intelligence dashboard providing comprehensive asset analysis across stocks and cryptocurrencies. Features radar charts for fundamental, technical, and sentiment analysis, price history with moving averages, sector comparisons, ESG profiling, and macro factor visualization. Presents complex market signals through an intuitive dark-themed interface with tabbed navigation across analysis dimensions.

In Progress

Thermofluidic Finance

A mathematical framework in which personal finance operates as a dynamical system governed by fluid mechanics and thermodynamics. Cash accounts behave as interconnected reservoirs of incompressible fluid. Volatile investments behave as compressible media subject to an exact First Law: unrealized gains are heat absorbed, realized gains are work extracted.

In Progress

On Dynamical Systems

A unifying mathematical framework for dynamical systems built on the product structure Behavior = Response × Drive. Parts I–IX develop the primordial pattern, potential energy and the three energy modes (potential, kinetic, dissipative), impedance and bond-graph power conjugacy, the complete cross-domain analogy, conservation laws and constitutive relations, spectral geometry of the state manifold, and a causal underdetermination theorem showing that behavior alone cannot resolve intrinsic response from extrinsic drive without auxiliary assumptions.

In Progress

Dynamic Control of an Aeropendulum

Advanced feedback control systems research using a propeller-driven pendulum testbed. Integrating sensor fusion, real-time control algorithms, and visualization to investigate nonlinear dynamics and control synthesis in physical systems.
