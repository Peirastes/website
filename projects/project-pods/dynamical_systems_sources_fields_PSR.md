## 1. Executive Summary

The Sources-Fields framework is a **unified mathematical theory of dynamical systems** presented in two complementary Quarto documents at 90% toward publication-ready status. Document 1 ("On Analogies") provides accessible exposition of the central principle: Behavior = Response × Drive across physics, engineering, biology, and psychology. Document 2 ("Sources, Fields, and the Architecture of Change") develops rigorous 9-part mathematical treatise with 11 appendices. The framework reveals that identical mathematics governs RLC circuits, mechanical oscillators, hydraulic systems, and behavioral dynamics. Core mathematical framework is sound with rigorous proofs in appendices. Main gaps are final editorial polish on Document 1 and verification of cross-domain parameter mapping consistency. Both documents are near publication-ready pending completion and peer review.

**Bottom Line:** Unified framework complete; 9-part treatise comprehensive; 90% toward publication with Document 1 polish and parameter table verification as remaining work.
