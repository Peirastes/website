The Allow-For and the Order of Prosecution

Two axes that keep getting welded together. One is symmetric and tells you where you stand. The other is asymmetric and tells you whether you earned it.

You may only condense as fast as you have widened.

Axis 1
Symmetric

Disposition — where you stand

The Allow-For paper draws this one directly: a neutral zone of tolerance, bounded on one side by methodical cynicism and on the other by methodical clinging to belief. The graphic is a practice limit keeping the open mind from wandering into either box.

It is symmetric on purpose. A lean is a lean, and the geometry has no preferred direction. Academic skepticism — “knowledge is impossible” — is a negative dogma in neutrality’s clothes, which is why the left wall is a wall and not an exit.

Axis 2
Asymmetric

Prosecution — the shape of the excavation

The second axis is not a position, it is an order of operations, and it has a picture: width is plurality, depth is commitment. The set of explanatory constructs still live is the mouth of the hole. How far you have committed is how deep you have dug.

Read that way, the three failures of inquiry are three excavation profiles, and the discipline is a rule about which dimension you are allowed to spend first.

Panels A and B end at the same depth. Nothing about the endpoint separates them — and either one could be a rejection or an acceptance, a landing on the left wall or the right wall of Figure 1. Axis 1 cannot grade the result. Only the profile can.

The hinge

Epoché is a clock, not a wall

“It refuses the comfortable, pre-loaded ‘simplest’ answer long enough for real inference to run.”TES-CMP-07 · Debriefing §1.6

That clause is the whole mechanism. Epoché is defined by a duration, which is what makes it the coupling between the two axes: it holds the Allow-For’s ground for exactly as long as the excavation needs to widen. Plot depth and width against inquiry time and the discipline becomes a single inequality.

Two stations

The bird and the flame are consecutive, not complementary

“Strongest available when concluding, weakest permitted when generating” describes two stations on the excavation, and the images carry one each. Each is a bilateral discipline — hold the bird without crushing it and without letting it go; kindle the flame without letting it go out and without burning the house down. Four failures, not two, and they arrange themselves.

Two structures fall out of the grid, and neither is visible when the images are taken one at a time.

The excess row is one failure at two scales. Crushing is over-commitment to a single construct. Burning the house down is what that becomes when the construct is left to grow — a conclusion that has become more important to protect than the integrity of the method, which is the Omega Hypothesis exactly (TES-COH-03), and panduction as its inference mode: falsifying everything except what the club already believes.

The deficiency row is one failure by two routes. Open your hands and the bird leaves; starve the flame and it goes out. Either way you no longer have it — but in both cases the thing itself is intact and someone else can still take it up.

That is the Voltaire tilt, and it is in the images after all: a released bird is alive, a crushed one is not. Be precise about where the tilt applies, though. It does not tilt Axis 1 — that spectrum stays symmetric. It tilts which error to risk while you are still holding.

Holding too long

The excavation stays wide past the point where you could have committed. The guilty man walks. Every construct is still live, still prosecutable, and a later independent line closes it.

Cost: time. Recoverable.

Closing too early

You condense before you widened. The innocent man hangs. The result looks like a proof and carries a proof’s confidence, and from the bottom of the shaft the excluded alternatives are not visible — so the error cannot be detected from inside.

Cost: the answer, silently. Unrecoverable.

Both are errors. They are not equally correctable, and that — not a preference for open-mindedness — is what sets the order. TES states the same tilt in one line, and it is worth having verbatim:

“Wrong answers under the right approach serve to inform. Right answers under the wrong approach result in an endless parade of paradox and naked emperors.”The Riddle of Certainty

TES-CRT-02
Canonical

The four zones, and the direction that does not pay

The excavation profiles are the same claim the Riddle of Certainty makes as a 2×2, and the original draws each zone as a rifle target — which fixes the vocabulary. Probative value is accuracy; reliability is precision. Precision comes free, by repeating the measurement. Accuracy has to be earned by finding the question that actually discriminates.

“Fewer and more spread detections are more valuable than deep but concentrated ones. Information should be rated on its reach and not simply its confidence.”The Riddle of Certainty

Which is the digging rule, in the source, almost word for word — spread before depth. And it is why one route across the square works and the other does not.

One coupling runs between the axes, and only in one direction: the shape of the hole is the fingerprint of the disposition. A mind that has already decided has nothing left to do but make its answer reliable, so it digs a shaft. That is sciebam exactly — conceive the answer, find inductive confirmation, neglect the alternatives, then police them. Conversely, holding plurality is what makes the mouth wide in the first place. Per hoc aditum and “dig wide first” are one instruction in two registers, one about mind and one about method.

The two guards
§1.6

Epoché is the ceiling. Bridgman is the floor.

Put the four failures next to the disposition spectrum and the spectrum stops being a second axis. Both of its walls are excess. Crushing is over-commitment toward yes; methodical cynicism is over-commitment toward no, which the doctrine says outright — a strong antithetical commitment wearing neutrality’s clothes, not a lack of belief. So Axis 1 is a cross-section of the excess row, showing which direction the crush pointed.

The deficiency row does not slice. There is no denial-flavoured way to lose a bird. Absence has no direction, which is why the two rows were never mirror images.

Which explains why §1.6 lists that pair against the Problem of Abduction. Parable-collecting is the deficiency failure that feels like diligence, and catching it takes both guards: epoché to keep you from seizing the first good parable, Bridgman to keep you from seizing the fortieth.

And the grid exposes an exploit worth carrying separately. We have just established that deficiency is the safe error — so anyone who wants to destroy a hypothesis unrecoverably has a costume ready. Do the excess; dress it as deficiency. Never run the line, and call it patience. Withhold assent, and call it rigour. The tilt we correctly prefer becomes cover for its own violation, and that is methodical cynicism exactly: an excess failure impersonating a deficiency one.

It also settles whether the never-closing inductive gap is infinite cover. It is — the tilt is the cover. The diagnostic holds anyway, and it is a behavioural one: honest deficiency runs the independent line when it is offered. Disguised excess finds a reason not to.

Provenance

Whose claim is whose

⚠ The Bridgman caveat — Debriefing §1.6

Bridgman and epoché are named there as the paired guards against the Problem of Abduction: “he who collects the most parables, wins.” Every picture on this page is the parable-form that section warns about. They are handles on something already comprehended, never substitutes for comprehending it, and never instruments to derive with.

Where the images still fail. The hole carries only the cost asymmetry, not the impossibility — physically you can always widen a hole, so the claim that Zone II does not traverse has to come from TES-CRT-01 and the bias/variance argument, not from the picture. And the convergence in Figure 4 — both images tilting the same way — is a reason to check the doctrine, not a result. It does check out: excess lands on the Omega Hypothesis and premature condensation, both named unrecoverable; deficiency lands on an unprosecuted hypothesis, which any later independent line can revive. But had it not checked out, the pictures agreeing would have counted for nothing. The moment an image starts doing the reasoning instead of transporting it, it is costing rather than paying.
