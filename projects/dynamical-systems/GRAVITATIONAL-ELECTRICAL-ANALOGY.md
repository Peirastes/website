Added comprehensive treatment of gravity and electromagnetism as parallel manifestations of the **Forces = Sources × Fields** principle. The key pedagogical innovation is **dimensional analysis** revealing that:

- **Electrical voltage:** V = J/C (energy per unit *charge*)

### 3. **Reveals Why Analogies Are Possible**

The framework now shows that analogies between domains are not just useful tricks—they're **mathematical necessities arising from conservation laws and power conjugacy.**

Proof structure:
1. Both gravity and electricity obey inverse-square laws
2. Both can be expressed as gradients of potentials
3. Both potentials satisfy Poisson equations with identical form

5. Therefore, they have identical mathematical structure

For gravity: "Gravitational potential = m²/s² ... what does that mean?"
Answer: **It means energy per kilogram = joules per kilogram—just like voltage is energy per coulomb.**

This immediately makes gravitational potential intuitive. A potential of 981 J/kg at height 100 m over Earth means "each kilogram has 981 joules of potential energy," just as a 1.5V battery means "each coulomb has 1.5 joules of potential energy."

**Part D: Power Analysis**
- Electrical: P = V × I where I is charge flow rate

## Key Educational Insights

### 1. **Dimensional Analysis as Revelation Tool**

The analogy is "revealed" by dimensional analysis, not mathematical formalism. This is powerful because:
- Dimensional analysis is simple and accessible
- It shows the analogy without needing to solve equations
- It works for high school students as well as physicists
- It emphasizes that the analogy is **mathematical necessity**, not clever trick

### 2. **Mass ↔ Charge Correspondence**

The deepest insight: **In the context of fields, mass and charge play identical mathematical roles.**

This means:
- Anything true about charge distributions applies to mass distributions
- Anything true about electric fields applies to gravitational fields
- Power calculations are identical
- Energy calculations are identical

### 3. **Why Coupling Constants Differ**

The constant $k_e = 8.99 \times 10^9$ N·m²/C² is vastly larger than $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}$ N·m²/kg².

Why? Because electricity is electromagnetic force (unified at quantum level), while gravity is geometric (spacetime curvature). At low velocities and weak fields, gravity still looks like a force, but its "coupling strength" is much weaker.

**This isn't a sign of fundamental difference—it's just about relative strength.**

### 4. **Universal Power Conjugacy**

Power has identical form everywhere:
- Electrical: V (volts) × I (amperes) = Watts
- Gravitational: φ (m²/s²) × dm/dt (kg/s) = Watts
- Mechanical: F (Newtons) × v (m/s) = Watts
- Thermal: T (Kelvin) × entropy flow (J/(K·s)) = Watts

This universality shows **power is the fundamental quantity**, not force or voltage or temperature—they're all just effort-flow pairs in different domains.

"You've all learned electrical potential. The voltage on a battery is energy per unit charge. Well, gravitational potential is energy per unit mass. That means gravitational potential is measured in joules per kilogram—or equivalently, meters-squared per second-squared.
