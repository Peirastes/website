## AI Agent Instructions

When populating this template:

1. **Match detail to significance.** Not every section requires extensive content—populate proportionally to what matters.

2. **Distinguish observation from inference.** Clearly separate what was measured/observed (Facts) from what was concluded/estimated (Patterns, Stories).

3. **Be explicit about confidence levels.** Flag certainty vs. estimation vs. assumption throughout.

4. **Preserve the epistemic trail.** Document not just conclusions but how they were reached—this enables meaningful review and revision.

5. **Prioritize informative error over hollow correctness.** If something isn't working or doesn't make sense, say so clearly. Vague hedging helps no one.

6. **Test claims against nulls.** Don't just argue for positions—define what would falsify them and check whether that's occurred.

7. **Update the knowledge state.** Each report should advance understanding of what's known, unknown, and uncertain—not just track tasks.

8. **Connect to the POD.** This report provides depth; the POD provides orientation. They should be consistent and complementary.
