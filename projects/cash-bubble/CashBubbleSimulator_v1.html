ABOUT THIS INSTRUMENT
Cash accounts behave as reservoirs of incompressible fluid governed by conservation laws. Investments behave as compressible gas bubbles whose expansion and compression follow thermodynamic principles.

ΔU = δQ − δW — The First Law of Thermodynamics applied to your portfolio: unrealized gains are heat input (δQ), realized gains are work extracted (δW).

Bubbles rise when profitable, sink when losing value. Their depth in the cylinder represents percent return — the surface is breakeven. Temperature controls market volatility. Inflation shifts the equilibrium.
